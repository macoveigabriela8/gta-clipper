# Picking a stream moment worth a Short

- **Sound-off test:** mute it. If a stranger still gets what happened within two seconds, keep it.
- **Setup, then payoff:** something is planned, promised or threatened, then reality answers (crash, betrayal, arrest, the big laugh). No payoff, no Short.
- **Window:** start 3-6 s before the setup begins; end 1-2 s after the reaction peaks. Aim for 15-45 s total.
- **Skip:** menus, loading screens, map browsing, long drives with nothing said, and stretches where music or radio plays (copyright claims).
- **Self-contained:** if it needs minutes of backstory to land, drop it. In a compilation, stay inside one streamer's clip.

## The hook (black pill, first 3 s)

5-9 words. Tell the viewer what is about to happen, never how it ends. Plain words, no emoji, no hashtags.
- **Stakes:** "He paid 25K for this truck"
- **Curiosity:** "Watch the cop's face"
- **Irony:** "He said nothing could go wrong"
- **Numbers:** "Third escape attempt in ten minutes"
- **POV:** "POV: your getaway driver is new"
- **Stakes + doubt:** "One wrong turn ends this heist"
